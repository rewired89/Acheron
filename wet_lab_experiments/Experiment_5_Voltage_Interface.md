# Experiment 5: Voltage Interface

**Full protocol:** `experiments/BENCH_PROTOCOL_BSUBTILIS.md`, Stage B4.

## The goal, plainly

Everything before this experiment re-tests things that are already
well-established in other labs. This is the first experiment that tests
something nobody, anywhere, has ever built: wiring the genetic switch's
output to an actual electrical channel in the cell membrane, so flipping the
gene switch also flips a real, measurable voltage.

This requires switching to a different bacterial strain than Experiments 1
through 4, because the one used so far doesn't form the biofilm structure
this part needs.

## What "pass" looks like

A real, repeatable difference in membrane voltage between the two written
states, in the same direction every time, big enough to tell apart from
ordinary cell-to-cell variation.

## What "fail" looks like, and why that's still valuable

This is the experiment most likely to fail, and it's worth running
precisely because a clean failure here is the single most useful result
available: it would mean the genetic memory works, but reading it out
electrically needs a different approach.

## Cost and time

A real step up from Experiments 1 through 4, new strain, new equipment. Not
yet quoted anywhere in this project.
