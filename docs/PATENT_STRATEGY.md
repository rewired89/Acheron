## Comparison: Levin's Science vs. Your Invention

### The Legal Principle

**You CANNOT patent:**
- Laws of nature
- Natural phenomena
- Abstract ideas

**You CAN patent:**
- **Practical applications** of natural phenomena (Diamond v. Chakrabarty, 1980)
- **New uses** of known materials (In re Schoenwald, 1992)
- **Engineered systems** based on biological principles (Association for Molecular Pathology v. Myriad Genetics, 2013)

### What Levin Discovered (Unpatentable)

❌ **Scientific fact:** Planarian bioelectric gradients control regeneration
❌ **Natural phenomenon:** Cells maintain voltage patterns
❌ **Published research:** BETSE simulation framework (open-source)

## Recommended Patent Claims Structure

### Claim 1 (Broadest - Method)

"A method for storing digital information in living tissue, comprising:
- (a) providing a plurality of somatic cells coupled via gap junctions;
- (b) encoding binary data as spatial patterns of membrane voltage potentials;
- (c) applying optogenetic stimulation to establish distinct voltage states representing binary '1' and '0';
- (d) allowing said voltage patterns to stabilize via bioelectric coupling;
- (e) reading said voltage patterns via voltage-sensitive imaging to retrieve said binary data."

**Why this works:**
- Broad enough to cover variations (doesn't limit to planarians only)
- Specific enough to be defensible (optogenetics + gap junctions + binary encoding)
- No prior art covers this exact sequence

### Claim 2 (Device)

"A bioelectric memory device comprising:
- a substrate containing living somatic cells derived from regenerative organisms;
- gap junction networks coupling said cells electrically;
- optogenetic actuators configured to induce voltage states in targeted cell clusters;
- voltage-sensing apparatus for reading bioelectric patterns;
- wherein said voltage patterns persist for at least 24 hours without active maintenance."

**Why this works:**
- Hardware claim (harder to design around than method)
- "Regenerative organisms" covers planarians without limiting to them
- 24-hour persistence claim is provable via your simulations

### Claim 3 (Software - Encoder)

"A bioelectric pattern encoder comprising:
- an input module receiving binary data;
- a spatial mapping algorithm assigning said binary data to cell coordinates;
- an optogenetic pulse generator producing wavelength-specific light patterns;
- a decoder module translating voltage distributions to binary output;
- wherein said encoder provides error correction via spatial redundancy."

**Why this works:**
- Software tied to hardware (DMD, optogenetics)
- Produces tangible result (voltage patterns)
- Not abstract (specific technical implementation)

### Claim 4 (Self-Healing Feature)

"The bioelectric memory device of Claim 2, wherein said somatic cells regenerate after physical damage, restoring said bioelectric patterns via morphogenetic memory."

**Why this works:**
- This is your KILLER FEATURE vs. Cortical Labs
- Levin showed regeneration but not for DATA STORAGE
- Combining regeneration + computing is novel
