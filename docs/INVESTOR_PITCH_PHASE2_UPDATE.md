## What is Bio-RAID?

### The Analogy (30-Second Explanation)

**Traditional RAID** (data centers):
- 10 hard drives working together
- If 1 drive fails → hot spare rebuilds data
- Manual replacement every 3-5 years

**Bio-RAID** (Acheron Phase 2):
- 20 living tissue chambers (tiles)
- If 1 tile degrades → neighbors fragment and re-seed it
- **Tiles regenerate indefinitely** (never need replacement)

**The Magic:** When you cut a planarian in half, BOTH pieces regenerate into complete worms - AND they remember the same bioelectric pattern. We use this to copy data.
