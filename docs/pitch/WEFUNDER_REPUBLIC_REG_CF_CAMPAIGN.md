# Acheron: Reg CF Equity Crowdfunding Campaign Draft (Wefunder / Republic)

**Read this note before using anything below.** A Reg CF raise is a securities
offering regulated by the SEC. What follows is draft campaign narrative only, the
kind of text that appears on a Wefunder or Republic project page. It is not, and
cannot substitute for, the actual offering documents: a Form C filing, audited or
reviewed financial statements (required above certain raise thresholds), a
subscription agreement, and defined security terms (SAFE, convertible note, or
priced equity, with a valuation cap or price per share). Those require a securities
attorney and must go through the funding portal's own compliance review before this
can legally go live. Bracketed placeholders below (raise target, minimum, security
type, valuation) are business and legal decisions for you and counsel to set, not
something to fill in speculatively here.

Given the project's own audit is explicit that the core science is unproven, full
disclosure of that fact in the risk factors section below is not optional. Omitting
or softening it in an actual securities offering is a real legal exposure
(material omission), not just a communications risk. This draft treats that
disclosure as load-bearing, not as boilerplate.

## The problem

Biocomputing today runs on cultured neurons (Cortical Labs, Koniku, FinalSpark):
volatile memory that needs continuous life support and cannot repair itself, with
typical operating lifespans of 6 months to 2 years. No company currently sells a
validated write/read interface for encoding arbitrary binary data into a
bioelectric or epigenetic cell state, in any organism. That gap is real, and it is
unclaimed.

## What Acheron has built

Not a working product. What we have is a rigorously stress-tested research
program that has already found and corrected its own early mistakes in public,
which we treat as the credibility asset rather than something to hide:

- **A falsified early claim, documented rather than buried.** Our original
  simulation showed a voltage pattern persisting passively in a cell for 24 hours.
  Re-running it with realistic biological noise sources collapsed that result to
  near-chance integrity. We do not present the original number as valid; our own
  internal audit records exactly where and why it broke.
- **A concrete, buildable replacement design.** A genetic toggle-switch circuit
  built from the original Gardner, Cantor and Collins (2000) synthetic bistable
  topology, combined with promoters from the Bacillus BioBrick Box, a free,
  characterized parts library matched to our target chassis, *Bacillus subtilis*.
  Simulating the combined design surfaced a specific, checkable engineering
  constraint (promoter strength matching) before any wet-lab money is spent.
- **A staged, falsifiable wet-lab validation plan**, ordered from cheapest to most
  expensive, each stage a clean go/no-go gate:

| Stage | Question answered | Est. cost | Est. time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Does the matched-promoter circuit show real bistability in living *B. subtilis*? | $2,000-$5,000 | 6-10 weeks |
| 1 | Do arbitrary voltage patterns persist 24h+ in a fast, simple gap-junction cell system? | $2,400-$10,000 | 3-6 months |
| 2 | Do arbitrary patterns persist in a regenerating organism? | $10,000-$30,000 | 6-12 months |
| 3 | Can the circuit's genetic output be coupled to a real, measurable membrane-voltage bit? | To be scoped with a synthetic-biology partner | TBD |

- **Three US patent applications filed** covering the core memory architecture,
  self-healing recovery, and environment-adaptive longevity, pending examination.

## Risk factors

This section is required in any real Reg CF offering, and we are treating it as
substantive rather than as a formality.

- **The core scientific premise is unproven.** We can honestly claim that
  simulation and literature support the plausibility of an engineered bistable
  circuit holding a data bit under realistic biological noise. We cannot claim
  that any of this has been demonstrated in living cells. Every dollar of the
  wet-lab budget above exists specifically to test that gap.
- **A negative result at any validation stage is a real possibility**, and by
  design, ends or redirects that line of work rather than being absorbed quietly.
  Investors should read the staged plan above as a series of go/no-go gates, not
  a guaranteed path to a product.
- **The voltage-interface link (Stage 3) has no prior art in any organism.**
  Even full success at Stages 0-2 does not guarantee this link is achievable.
- **Pre-revenue, single-founder-led technical team.** The company's technical
  work currently depends on one lead researcher; wet-lab execution depends on
  securing external lab partners, which is not yet contracted.
- **Long, uncertain R&D timeline.** Even a fully successful validation path
  described above is a foundational science result, not a shippable product; a
  commercial memory substrate remains multiple additional R&D phases away.
- **Competitive and adjacent-IP risk.** Morphoceuticals (bioelectric regenerative
  medicine, not computing) and BioCompute Inc. (DNA-sequence data storage in
  bacteria, a different storage mechanism) both work in adjacent space; a full
  freedom-to-operate analysis against their IP has not been completed.
- **Patents are filed, not granted.** All three applications are pending USPTO
  examination; none has yet issued as a granted patent.
- **Regulatory and biosafety considerations.** Work with engineered microorganisms
  is subject to institutional biosafety and, depending on jurisdiction and future
  organism choices, additional regulatory oversight not yet fully scoped.
